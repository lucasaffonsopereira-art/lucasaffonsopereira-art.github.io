I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Mathematics and Statistics of the University of São Paulo (IME-USP), currently visiting NYU Shanghai and working with Roberto Fernández.

I received my PhD in Applied Mathematics from the University of São Paulo. My doctoral work developed multiscale contour methods for long-range Ising models and a boundary-condition approach to quantum statistical mechanics.
